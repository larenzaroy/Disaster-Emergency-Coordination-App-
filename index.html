Disaster Emergency Checklists

Flash Flood Protocol

Immediate Action: Move to higher elevation. Disconnect electrical switches. Do not walk or drive through moving water (>15cm can sweep you off feet).

  • Keep emergency medications in sealed waterproof bag
  • Signal rescue boat ambulances with torch or bright cloth
  • Boil all drinking water or use halogen tablets
Earthquake Protocol

Immediate Action: DROP, COVER, AND HOLD ON under sturdy table or interior corner. Avoid glass, windows, and outer walls.

  • If trapped under debris: Tap on pipes/walls instead of shouting to conserve oxygen
  • Cover nose and mouth with cloth against concrete dust
  • Turn off domestic gas cylinder valve immediately
Fire & Smoke Protocol

Immediate Action: Stay low below the smoke line. Smoke rises to ceiling. Check doorknobs with the back of your hand before opening.

  • Wet a cloth and place over nose and mouth
  • If clothes catch fire: STOP, DROP, AND ROLL
  • Never use elevators during a fire; use fire escape stairwells
CPR & Cardiac Protocol

Immediate Action: Call 1-Tap SOS immediately. Place hands in center of chest and push hard and fast (100–120 compressions/min) to the beat of "Stayin' Alive".

  • Push down at least 5 cm (2 inches) depth
  • Allow chest to fully recoil between each compression
  • If AED available, turn on and follow automated voice prompts
